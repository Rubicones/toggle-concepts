# Moss & Co.
Online shop of low-maintenance indoor plants delivered in compostable pots, with a care app that reminds you when to water.

- Audience: 25–40, renters with small flats, "I kill every plant" people.
- Offer: starter set of 3 plants €39, free delivery this week, 60-day "it survives or we replace it" promise.
- Tone: friendly, reassuring, light humour about plant guilt.
- Colours: moss green #4A6741, clay #C9784E, paper #FAF7F0.
- Show: real plants, hands, small apartments. Avoid: jungle clichés, monstera overload.
