# Ledgerly
Invoicing and expense app for freelancers in the EU. Snap a receipt, it is categorised; send an invoice in 30 seconds; see what you owe in tax.

- Audience: freelancers and sole traders, designers, developers, translators.
- Offer: free for the first 10 invoices, then €7/month. Promo code NIGHTSHIFT gives 3 months free.
- Proof: 40 000 users, 4.8 rating in App Store.
- Tone: clear, calm, competent. Fintech without the cold blue cliché.
- Colours: ink #1B1B3A, mint #7BE0AD, off-white #F7F7F2.
- Must show the app UI (invoice screen) at least once. No stock photos of laptops in cafés.
