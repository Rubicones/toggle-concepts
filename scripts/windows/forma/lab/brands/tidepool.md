# Tidepool Festival
Three-day electronic and ambient music festival on the Adriatic coast, 18–20 July. Sunrise sets on the beach, small stages, 4 000 people max.

- Audience: 23–38, people who left big festivals because of crowds.
- Offer: early-bird pass €119 until 31 March (regular €159). Line-up announcement this Friday.
- Tone: dreamy, intimate, a bit mysterious. Not EDM-party loud.
- Colours: deep teal #0B3C49, coral #FF7F6B, sand #EADBC8.
- Show: water, light, small crowds, sunrise. Avoid: neon lasers, massive main stages.
