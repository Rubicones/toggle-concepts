# Grit Gym
24/7 strength gym chain in Belgrade, 5 locations. No mirrors-and-selfies culture: plates, chalk, coaches who write your program.

- Audience: 25–45, people who want to get strong, not "toned"; many beginners afraid of the free-weights area.
- Offer: first month 2 900 RSD instead of 5 900 RSD, free intro session with a coach.
- Tone: direct, encouraging, short sentences. No body shaming, no before/after photos.
- Colours: black #0E0E0E, chalk white #F2F0EA, signal orange #FF5A1F.
- Typeface: condensed bold sans for headlines.
- Languages: Serbian (Latin) is primary, English secondary.
