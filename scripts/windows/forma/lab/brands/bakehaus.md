# Bakehaus
Neighbourhood sourdough bakery with 3 shops in Vienna. Bread baked overnight, cinnamon knots on weekends, coffee from a local roaster.

- Audience: locals 25–60 on the way to work; weekend families.
- Offer: new "morning box" — loaf + 2 pastries + coffee for €9.90, pre-order in the app until 22:00, pick up from 7:00.
- Tone: warm, crafty, honest. A bit of Viennese dryness.
- Colours: crust brown #8B5A2B, flour white #FBF8F3, rye black #222.
- Languages: German primary, English secondary.
- Must mention pick-up time. Never use the word "artisan".
