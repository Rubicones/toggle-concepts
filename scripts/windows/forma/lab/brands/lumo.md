# Lumo
Oat milk for night owls. Lumo is a calm, slightly sleepy oat drink made for people who work, study or create late at night. Barista-grade, foams well, no added sugar.

- Audience: 22–35, freelancers, students, night-shift workers in big cities.
- Offer: new 1 L carton, launch price €2.49, in Lidl and Billa from March.
- Tone: soft, warm, a little witty. Never "energy drink" loud.
- Colours: midnight blue #14213D, moon cream #F4EBD9, warm amber #F2A541.
- Logo: lowercase wordmark "lumo" with a small crescent over the "u".
- Must say: "No added sugar". Must not say: "healthy", "dairy-free milk" (legal wording is "oat drink").
