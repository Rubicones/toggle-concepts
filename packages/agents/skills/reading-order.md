## Principles
Decide the reading order before you build: one thing the viewer takes in first, a second that explains it, a third that tells them what to do. Everything else supports these three or goes. Size, contrast, position and space carry the order; colour alone does not. When two elements compete for first, change one of them until it clearly yields.

## Checks
- Is one element read first by its size, contrast or position?
- Is there one element read second that explains the first?
- Is the call to action read last?
- Is every other element smaller or lower in contrast than the first?

## Signs for the reviewer
Two elements of similar size and contrast compete for first.
The order depends on colour alone.
