## Principles
A generated picture is worth its cost only when it shows something the frame needs and code cannot draw: the product in use, a person in the named moment, a real material or place.

Start from what the picture must prove, in one sentence. If the sentence could be illustrated by a generic object or an empty space, the picture is not needed or the idea is not ready.

Write the brief as a photographer's shot, concretely: who or what is in it and what they are doing; where; the camera's distance and angle; where the subject sits in the frame and how much of it it fills; the direction and quality of light; the background and what must be absent; materials and colours, taken from the brand's own palette and materials where they belong to the scene. Adjectives of mood are not a brief.

Stage for the layout. Decide before the brief where the headline and the mark will sit, and ask for a calm area there; keep the subject clear of edges so every format can crop it; one camera angle per scene.

Keep the brand's things real. The product, the interface and the marks come from the supplied files, as they are or as references; never ask a picture to invent them, and never ask for words, numbers or logos inside a picture.

Ask for few pictures and use each fully. One strong picture, large, does more than several small ones. A stand-in is acceptable only when it already shows the real subject at draft quality; a shadow or an empty shape where the subject will be is not a draft of the idea.

## Checks
- Does each generated picture show something code cannot draw: the product in use, a person, a real material or place?
- Is every generated picture free of words, numbers and logos?
- Does the picture leave a calm area where the headline and the mark sit?
- Is the picture's subject clear of the edges, so every format can crop it?

## Signs for the reviewer
A generic object or an empty scene stands where the subject should be.
Several small pictures where one large picture would do.
Lettering or a mark appears inside a generated picture.
