## Principles
When you revise, name the finding or what the render shows, then change the least that fixes it. Keep what already works exactly as it is. Do not restart a frame to fix one part, and do not change two things at once when one explains the problem. After the change, check that the fix holds and nothing else moved.

## Checks
- Is the render free of earlier notes that you now rule open or worse?
- Is the render free of checks that passed in your earlier review and fail now?
- Is the render free of a new problem caused by the fix for another note?

## Signs for the reviewer
A fix for one part changed the whole frame.
A finding that was fixed has come back elsewhere.
