---
id: revise-with-reason
title: Revise with a reason
purpose: Change only what a finding or a render shows is wrong, and keep what already works.
version: 1
roles: designer,creative-code
---
When you revise, name the finding or what the render shows, then change the least that fixes it. Keep what already works exactly as it is. Do not restart a frame to fix one part, and do not change two things at once when one explains the problem. After the change, check that the fix holds and nothing else moved.
