## 2. Summary of Changes

### 2.1 What changed

- `packages/director/quality.ts` (new): everything of critic@3.
  - `QUALITY_CHECKS`, `reviewMaterialFor`: seven hero checks and one ideas check, added after each skill's own checks. Earlier checks keep their numbers. The skill files are not edited, so version 5 runs freeze the same skills as before.
  - `heroHasCallToAction`: when the hero scene's words hold no call to action, the two checks that ask about one are left out of the version 6 hero review.
  - `HERO_REVIEW_INSTRUCTIONS_V3`, `SELECTION_INSTRUCTIONS_V3`: the reviewer's instructions.
  - `strictRound`: the app also applies the strict rule. A yes whose evidence is hedged becomes a no and a P1 note for the designer.
  - `chosenIdeaNote`: the chosen idea, in the designer's own words, for the hero review.
  - `reasonOnly`: the selection's reason without sentences that tell the designer how to build.
  - `NOTES_INSIDE_THE_IDEA`, `lastTurnNote`: what the designer reads with notes.
- `packages/director/critic-review.ts`: `settleHeroReview` takes the strict rule; with it, a review stops as "all pass" only when the reviewer also accepts the frame. Without it nothing changes.
- `apps/local-runner/director/hero-chat-loop.ts`:
  - The last turn. When the review ends (accepted, out of rounds, or no progress) with points still open and a turn is left, the designer gets one turn for them. There is no further review. If that turn's code does not build, one repair turn follows; then the frame is accepted.
  - The notes message carries the rule to solve notes inside the chosen idea.
  - The selection's reason is passed through `reasonOnly`.
- `packages/director/designer.ts`: `hero.lastTurn` in the saved state, so a restart keeps it.
- `apps/local-runner/director/worker.ts`, `scripts/bench/v5-direction.ts`: the selection and the hero review use the critic@3 instructions, checks, chosen idea and strict rule when the direction is version 6.

**Where the selection's building advice came from.** The reviewer's role line tells it to "route precise notes to the director". In run 2 the selection's reason ended with "Route to the director: keep … and make sure …". Version 6 asks for a reason without advice and also drops such sentences before the designer reads the reason.

### 2.2 Every changed text, before and after (version 6 only; for the owner's approval)

**Text 1 — the hero review's instructions.**

Before (critic@2, unchanged for version 5):

```text
<the shared opening> This is the hero frame, the frame every other scene follows: judge stopping power and the idea first, then craft.
1. When your earlier review is given, rule on each earlier note first: fixed, open or worse.
2. Answer every check yes or no, with one line of evidence from the render (s = skill id, n = check number).
3. Then add new notes. Each names its skill and check, says what to change and why, has a priority (P0 blocks, P1 should, P2 could) and names the view. A note that contradicts an earlier one names it in replaces and says why it no longer holds; otherwise replaces is empty.
accepted is true when every check passes and no P0 or P1 note is open. Approved words and facts stay as they are.
```

After (critic@3):

```text
<the shared opening> This is the hero frame, the frame every other scene follows: judge stopping power and the idea first, then craft. You see it at full size and at thumbnail size.
1. When your earlier review is given, rule on each earlier note first: fixed, open or worse.
2. Answer every check yes or no, with one line of evidence from the render (s = skill id, n = check number). Yes means the thing is plainly true of this frame as a stranger sees it. When your evidence needs a reservation (it is implied, partly so, acceptable, or the check does not apply), the answer is no.
3. Then add new notes. Each names its skill and check, has a priority (P0 blocks, P1 should, P2 could) and names the view. In problem, say what a viewer sees or fails to understand. In fix, say what must become true for the viewer and what must stay as it is. The solution is the designer's: a fix names no position, no added element, no arrangement and no other idea. A note that contradicts an earlier one names it in replaces and says why it no longer holds; otherwise replaces is empty.
accepted is true when every check passes, no P0 or P1 note is open, and you would present this frame to the client as it is. When every check passes and you would still not present it, accepted is false and a note says what is missing. Approved words and facts stay as they are.
```

## 3. Self-Review & Risk Audit ("Can It Produce Another Bug or Blocking Error?")

1. **Frozen `config_hash`, `requestId`, `quoteId` on active runs:** no. Every text, check and rule is behind `designerVersion >= 6`. The version 5 rehearsal asserts that its hero review holds none of them; the version 5 hero task, digests and stage E opening are still pinned by hash.
2. **`DRAFT_INPUT_CAP` and schema limits:** the hero review grows by about 340 tokens plus the chosen idea (at most about 400). The review schema is unchanged.
3. **`AGENTS.md` principles:** no template, layout, example style or name in any text. No paid call. Approved copy is untouched. The last turn spends a designer turn inside the hero stage's own cap and no reviewer request.

Risks that remain:

- **Two checks describe a property of the frame, not only a fault.** "One element cropped by the frame, or at least twice the size of the next largest" and "the mark aligned to an edge or axis" are the measurable form of "a bold decision" and "the mark belongs". They name no layout, but they do push every hero frame the same way. The owner should weigh these two first.
- **The strict rule can fail a good frame.** The app turns a yes into a no on words such as "though", "implied", "acceptable", "not applicable", "partly". A reviewer that writes "though" in honest evidence loses the check. The designer is told which check and why.
- **A check that does not apply is a no under the strict rule.** So the two existing checks about a call to action are not asked when the hero scene's approved words hold none (as in the export's hero). The other checks keep their numbers. In that case version 6 asks 41 hero checks, not 43.
- **More failed checks mean the review uses both rounds more often.** The hero then ends by "reviews used" with a last turn, not by "all pass". The cost per hero should rise by about one designer turn.
- **`reasonOnly` drops sentences by their first word** ("Route", "Keep", "Make sure", "Add", "Use", …). A reason whose every sentence starts that way is kept whole rather than emptied.
- **The last turn is not reviewed.** Its result is accepted as it is, as a frame accepted at the turn cap is today.
- **Not tried live.** Whether the reviewer obeys "no arrangement in a fix" is unknown until run 3.
